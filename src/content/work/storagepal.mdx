## Cache what customers share

The catalog repeated the same database work on every visit. I cut queries and added indexes. Then I built the page on the server from cached data.

<div class="post-split not-prose">
  <div class="card fx">
    <p class="post-split-label"><Icon name="ph:database" /> Shared by everyone: cached</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Product data, stored per language</li>
      <li>Cleared when staff change the catalog</li>
      <li>Expires after 24 hours to catch anything missed</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="post-split-note">Some import paths do not clear the cache yet. The 24-hour expiry covers them.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card fx" style="--d:70ms">
    <p class="post-split-label"><Icon name="ph:user" /> Personal: read fresh</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Cart quantities, merged in on every request</li>
      <li>Custom items, kept out of the shared cache</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

## Sort out what is owed before charging

Unpaid amounts carried over into later invoices. The system could still treat them as separate debts, so more retries could charge the wrong amount.

<figure class="post-flow card not-prose fx">
  <ol>
    <li class="post-flow-node"><span class="post-icon"><Icon name="ph:receipt" /></span><strong>Invoice 1</strong><span>Left unpaid.</span></li>
    <li class="post-flow-link"><Icon name="px-arrow-right" /><span>carried into</span></li>
    <li class="post-flow-node"><span class="post-icon"><Icon name="ph:receipt" /></span><strong>Invoice 2</strong><span>Includes the unpaid amount.</span></li>
    <li class="post-flow-link"><Icon name="px-arrow-right" /><span>carried into</span></li>
    <li class="post-flow-node"><span class="post-icon"><Icon name="ph:check-circle" /></span><strong>Current invoice</strong><span>Holds the full amount owed.</span></li>
  </ol>
  <p class="post-flow-caps">One rule: the current invoice, at the head of the chain, is what the customer owes.</p>
</figure>

<div class="post-media not-prose fx">
  <div class="post-screen-img"><Image src={checkout} alt="StoragePal payment page with all payment details together" widths={[240, 480]} sizes="200px" /></div>
  <div>
    <h3>Payment rules</h3>
    <ul class="post-checks">
      <li>Charge the current invoice, and nothing else</li>
      <li>Never start a second payment while one is in progress</li>
      <li>Retry failed payments, and send blocked ones to a review queue</li>
      <li>Check the payment state again before each reminder</li>
    </ul>
    <dl class="post-figures"><div><dt>3 → 1</dt><dd>pages in the payment flow</dd></div></dl>

## Price transport by the real job

Quotes used to be flat by region. Now one engine prices each job from its real details. Customer care and logistics helped shape the rules.

<div class="post-principles not-prose">
  <div class="card post-before fx">

    <div class="post-before-lines" aria-hidden="true"><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span><span></span></div>

## Keep AI proposals inside the business rules

The shopping assistant turns a description or a photo into a proposed cart. The model helps. The business rules stay in charge.
