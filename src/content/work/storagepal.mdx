## A business already in operation

StoragePal was already running when I joined. Customers were booking pickups and paying invoices, and staff relied on the platform every day. I had to improve it while keeping that work moving.

I handle the work day to day: deciding what comes next, working out how it should work, writing and testing the code, and releasing it. If something breaks in production, I fix it.

I also built the [SPS warehouse app](/work/sps/) end to end, for the staff who run the warehouse.

## Cache what customers share

The catalog was doing the same database work for every visitor. I reduced the queries and added indexes, then cached the product data everyone shares. Each customer’s cart still loads separately.

## Make payment retries safe

A customer shouldn’t have to wonder whether clicking Pay again will charge them twice. I rebuilt the payment flow around the current amount due, with checks to make retries safe.

<div class="post-media not-prose fx">
  <div class="post-screen-img"><Image src={checkout} alt="StoragePal payment page with all payment details together" widths={[240, 480]} sizes="200px" /></div>
  <div>
    <h3>Payment rules</h3>
    <ul class="post-checks">
      <li>Charge only the current invoice</li>
      <li>Never start a second payment while one is in progress</li>
      <li>Retry failed payments and flag blocked payments for review</li>
      <li>Check the payment status before each reminder</li>
    </ul>
    <dl class="post-figures"><div><dt>3 <span class="to">→</span> 1</dt><dd>pages in the payment flow</dd></div></dl>

## Price transport by the real job

A flat regional price couldn’t account for the distance, crew, or access a job needed. I worked with customer support and logistics to turn those details into pricing rules.

## Keep AI proposals inside the business rules

Customers can describe what they want to store or send a photo. The assistant suggests a cart they can edit. I keep the item checks and pricing on the server, so the model can’t invent a product or set its price.
