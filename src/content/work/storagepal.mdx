## The setup

StoragePal collects customers' belongings, stores them, and brings them back on request. The platform is the whole business: catalogue, pricing, bookings, warehouse stock, invoicing, payments and the backoffice the team uses all day.

I lead the technical side: I decide what gets built and how, build a large part myself, and direct **Monder** (reporting, CRM, lead capture, reservations), **Med** (WordPress site, SEO, tools) and two testers who verify every feature before it ships.

## The transport pricing engine

Quotes used coarse regions and priced the two directions differently. I replaced that with one engine: real driving distance from Google Maps, the vehicle chosen from the cart contents, the cheapest crew, floors and lift.

## An AI cart builder in production

Customers describe their move in plain words or send photos. A Gemini assistant turns that into a priced cart, every item checked against the real catalogue, made-to-measure items priced by the same formula checkout uses. **42% of conversations end in a confirmed cart.**

## Billing that collects the right amount

I found a fault where unpaid months carried into the next invoice were collected more than once. I fixed it, then rebuilt collection: Stripe-rule retries, bank-transfer settlement, write-offs, partial payments, reminders under French consumer law. **77 failed payments recovered** in the first seven weeks, and the overdue figure the business relied on turned out to be wrong by a **factor of 13**.

## The warehouse link

The [SPS warehouse app](/work/sps/) runs on live order data through a REST API I designed: orders, returns, order changes with Stripe repricing, and retries that never charge twice. **835 orders** and **8,600+ status changes** processed.

## How it was built

Specs first, then Claude Code implements inside project rules that encode the business invariants (which invoice amounts may be summed, which permissions file every endpoint registers in). Hooks block destructive commands and lint every edit. I review every change; the testers verify it in the real app.
