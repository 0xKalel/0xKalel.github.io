## My role

I lead two developers and two testers, set the technical roadmap and build features. The platform manages bookings, storage and payments.

Orders grew **69%** and ad spend per order fell **22%**. Development, advertising and sales all contributed.

## Faster booking

I cut catalogue load time from **9.1 s to 0.9 s** and checkout from **7 steps to 3**. The checkout has processed **700+ orders** since December 2025.

The benchmark used the same machine and database, with 15 warm runs per version.

## Simpler shopping

I built a Gemini assistant that turns text or photos into a priced cart. **42% of conversations end in a confirmed cart.**

## Correct billing

I fixed duplicate billing and rebuilt payment recovery. The new process recovered **77 failed payments in seven weeks**.
