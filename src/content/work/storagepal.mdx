## Cache what customers share

The catalog ran the same database queries for every visitor. I cut the number of queries and added indexes before adding the cache.

## Make payment retries safe

A customer shouldn’t have to wonder whether clicking Pay again will charge them twice. I rebuilt the payment flow around the current amount due.

<div class="post-media not-prose fx">
  <div class="post-screen-img"><Image src={checkout} alt="StoragePal payment page with all payment details together" widths={[240, 480]} sizes="200px" /></div>
  <div>
    <h3>Payment rules</h3>
    <ul class="post-checks">
      <li>Charge only the current invoice</li>
      <li>Never start a second payment while one is in progress</li>
      <li>Retry failed payments and flag blocked payments for review</li>
      <li>Check the payment status before each reminder</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

## Keep AI proposals inside the business rules

Customers can ask the AI shopping assistant to suggest a cart. I keep the item checks and pricing on the server, so the model can’t invent a product or set its price.

## Upgrade with no maintenance window

In November 2026 I spent about two and a half weeks moving the platform off years-old software. I worked with AI coding agents and reviewed every change, but ran each cloud and DNS step myself.

  <div class="card post-no fx">

    <ul>
      <li>PHP 7.2, past end of life, on Neos Flow 5.2</li>
      <li>A server set up by hand in 2020</li>
      <li>The marketing site on a separate WordPress server</li>
      <li>API keys in git</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="card fx" style="--d:70ms">
    <p class="post-split-label"><Icon name="ph:check-circle" aria-hidden="true" /> After</p>

      <li><strong>PHP 8.5</strong> on Neos Flow 9.2, with MariaDB 11.4</li>
      <li>New AWS servers built from a script</li>
      <li>Marketing site, booking website, and staff admin tool on one host, with marketing pages as files in git</li>
      <li>Secrets out of git, and a scanner in CI that blocks them</li>
