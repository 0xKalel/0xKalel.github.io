## Connect the booking to the warehouse floor

Staff sorted out mismatched bookings by email, and they didn’t always record where items went. Now they do both in the app as they work.

I also maintain the StoragePal platform the app connects to. If a problem crosses between the two, I can fix both sides.

## Remove complexity the warehouse does not need

I first built offline support. Then I checked where the app would run: every warehouse had internet, so I took offline support out before daily use began.

## Keep work moving across system boundaries

Staff sometimes need to label an item before the platform approves a booking change. Making them wait would hold up the warehouse, so the app lets them print the label first.
