## The problem

Customers book storage online; movers bring their items to a warehouse and later bring them back. Before SPS, the warehouse had no reliable link to what was booked: staff tracked discrepancies through emails and memory, damage was billed inconsistently, and finding an item meant searching the building.

## How I checked the app before release

I used Claude Code during development and reviewed its changes against the project’s requirements. Checks include **659 PHPUnit tests**, Larastan level 5 static analysis, and **65 browser scenarios** run through Chrome DevTools MCP. These cover both successful workflows and failures, with findings recorded in a report.

I also worked with two testers who checked each workflow on the warehouse floor. That helped us verify the app in the setting where staff actually use it.

The project includes **1,110 commits**, with about 48,000 lines of PHP and 42,000 lines of React code.
