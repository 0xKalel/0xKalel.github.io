## The problem

Customers book storage online; movers bring their items to a warehouse and later bring them back. Before SPS, the warehouse had no reliable link to what was booked: discrepancies lived in email and memory, damage was billed inconsistently, and finding an item meant searching the building.

## Quality with AI in the loop

I built it with Claude Code as a pair programmer, and invested in making its output trustworthy: **659 PHPUnit tests** and Larastan level 5, **65 browser scenarios the agent runs itself** through Chrome DevTools MCP (recording every network, console, database and log error into a report), project rules loaded every session, and two testers I directed verifying each flow on the warehouse floor.

**1,110 commits**: about 48k lines of PHP and 42k lines of React.
