## What I built

Warehouse staff tracked booking errors through email and memory. Finding an item meant searching the building.

I built a phone app that connects bookings to the warehouse floor. Staff scan items in, record their location and photograph them before return.

## A failure I fixed

A sync bug marked **2,533 orders as cancelled**. Only **306** were cancelled. I traced it to four interacting bugs in filtering, date ranges and recovery.

I added retries, blocked deletion checks on incomplete data and added nightly reconciliation. Eight regression tests cover the fixes.

## Testing

**659 automated tests**, **65 browser scenarios** and two testers checking workflows on the warehouse floor.
