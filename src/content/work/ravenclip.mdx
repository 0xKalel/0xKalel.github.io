## Start with a complete workflow

RavenClip started with something I wanted to use myself. I built a version that could take a story all the way to a finished video, then worked on getting it to run without me starting each step.

<div class="card post-solo not-prose fx">
  <p class="eyebrow">The first weeks</p>
  <ol class="post-timeline">
    <li><b>First version:</b> a complete video, with each stage started by hand.</li>
    <li class="is-hit"><b>Week three:</b> it ran on a schedule and published on its own.</li>
    <li><b>Around then:</b> I added manual story selection and removed it the same day.</li>
  </ol>
</div>

I wanted it to keep publishing while I was busy with other work. A story picker wasn’t helping with that, so I took it back out.

## Recover without repeating work

If a render fails, I don’t want to pay for the narration and images again. Each video saves its progress so it can pick up where it stopped.

<PipelineRaven />

<div class="post-cards is-2 not-prose">

## Monitor backup providers

The videos kept coming, so I thought the providers were fine. One had actually been out of credit for 25 days. The backup was doing the work at a higher price.

<Potion id="ravenclip" />

<aside class="post-incident card not-prose fx">
  <div class="post-incident-head">
    <span class="post-icon"><Icon name="ph:detective" aria-hidden="true" /></span>
    <div>
      <p class="eyebrow">Production incident</p>
      <p class="post-incident-title">A backup provider hid an empty credit balance for 25 days</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <dl class="post-figures">
    <div><dt>25 days</dt><dd>provider out of credit</dd></div>
    <div><dt>8,162</dt><dd>calls sent to a more expensive backup service</dd></div>

  <div class="post-incident-foot">
    <p class="eyebrow">The fix</p>
    <ol class="post-fix">
      <li>Provider health checks and clear failure alerts.</li>

## Know the limits of AI checks

I can check whether a script stays on topic or fits the required length. I can’t treat a passing check as proof that every claim is true. That’s why customers can review videos and flagged ones wait for approval.

<div class="post-split not-prose">
  <div class="card fx">
    <p class="post-split-label"><Icon name="ph:check-circle" aria-hidden="true" /> Checked</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Script structure and length</li>
      <li>The script stays on topic</li>
      <li>The approved narration is not changed later</li>
      <li>On-screen figures come from data sources, not the model</li>
      <li>Failed drafts get specific instructions for the next attempt</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="card post-no fx" style="--d:70ms">

    </ul>
