## Start with a complete workflow

I wanted a simpler way to follow the news myself, so I built the whole flow first.

<div class="card post-solo not-prose fx">
  <p class="eyebrow">The first weeks</p>
  <ol class="post-timeline">
    <li><b>First version:</b> a complete video, with each stage started by hand.</li>
    <li class="is-hit"><b>Week three:</b> it ran on a schedule and published on its own.</li>
    <li><b>Around then:</b> I built a manual story picker, and removed it the same day.</li>
  </ol>
</div>

The priority was a channel that keeps publishing while its owner is busy. More controls could come once that worked.

## Choose architecture around recovery needs

A video passes through several paid services. Restarting after one failed step would throw away finished work and pay for it twice.

<PipelineRaven />

<div class="post-cards is-2 not-prose">
  <div class="card fx"><span class="post-icon"><Icon name="ph:floppy-disk" /></span><h3>Saved stages</h3><p>Each stage saves its status. A runner moves forward whatever is ready.</p></div>
  <div class="card fx" style="--d:70ms"><span class="post-icon"><Icon name="ph:lock-simple" /></span><h3>One worker per stage</h3><p>Database locks stop two workers from taking the same stage.</p></div>
  <div class="card fx"><span class="post-icon"><Icon name="ph:recycle" /></span><h3>Reuse on retry</h3><p>A failed render reuses the assets it already has.</p></div>
  <div class="card fx" style="--d:70ms"><span class="post-icon"><Icon name="ph:seal-check" /></span><h3>Publish once</h3><p>Publishing has its own guard against duplicate posts.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="decision-note not-prose">
  <p class="eyebrow">When I skip it</p>
  <p>All this means more state and recovery code to maintain. For a short rebuild that a user starts, a simple job chain is enough.</p>
</div>

## Notice when fallback hides a problem

Fallback kept videos coming, so nothing looked wrong.

  <div class="post-incident-foot">

      <li>A daily check outside the job queue, so it still reports when the workers are down.</li>

## Define what AI checks can prove

Automatic checks catch a lot, but not everything. So customers keep the final say.

<div class="post-split not-prose">
  <div class="card fx">
    <p class="post-split-label"><Icon name="ph:check-circle" /> Checked</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Script structure and length</li>
      <li>The story stays about its subject</li>
      <li>The approved narration is not changed later</li>
      <li>On-screen figures come from data sources, not the model</li>
      <li>Retries are told exactly what to fix</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="card post-no fx" style="--d:70ms">
    <p class="post-split-label"><Icon name="ph:warning-circle" /> Not proven</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Every factual claim.</strong> So customers keep review controls, and flagged videos wait for approval.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
