## What I built

I built RavenClip for businesses that need short videos without a video team. It started as a tool for following news and reached paying customers four months after the first commit.

It selects a story, writes the script, adds narration and visuals, and publishes to TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Customers can review videos before publishing. Instagram approval is pending.
